Privacy & limitations

The local server is HTTP (not encrypted). Use only on a trusted private Wi-Fi. Your secret token is never stored in this browser and is sent only to the phone when you connect. Avoid public Wi-Fi.

Your phone must stay on, the foreground server must be active, and compatible AI model weights must be imported in RealFrame. Wan video can exceed mobile RAM. This web portal does not make your phone publicly accessible over the internet; remote internet needs a separately secured VPN or HTTPS reverse proxy.
